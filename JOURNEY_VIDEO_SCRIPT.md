### 💡 Tips for this Faceless Format in Google Vids:

2. **Dynamic AI Voices:** If you aren't recording yourself, use a high-quality AI voiceover tool (like ElevenLabs or Google Vids built-in text-to-speech) for consistent pacing.
3. **Keep it Moving:** Make sure every 8-second clip has movement, whether it's an animation, a screen recording, or dynamic text.
