01. Executive Summary & Abstract

Across Tamil Nadu’s 38 districts and over 16,000 revenue villages, valuable government poramboke lands and waterbodies (Eri, Kulam, Meikkal, Salai) face relentless illegal construction. Current monitoring depends entirely on manual foot inspections by Village Administrative Officers (VAOs) or delayed citizen petitions, resulting in detection latencies ranging from 6 months to 3 years. By that time, multi-story reinforced concrete structures are completed, leading to lengthy civil court stay orders and expensive eviction litigation.

GEOMMAND re-engineers this reactive model into an autonomous space-to-ground-to-court pipeline. The system ingests 5-day optical satellite imagery (Sentinel-2 MSI) and radar feeds (Sentinel-1 SAR), applies normalized spectral index differencing (NDBI and NDVI), isolates candidate footprints with computer vision, filters out private Patta lands via PostGIS spatial intersection, and dispatches geofenced mobile verification orders to the field VAO. Crucially, the system dynamically reconstructs certified FMB (Field Measurement Book) Encroachment Sketches and generates statutory show-cause notices under Section 7 and eviction warrants under Section 6 of the Tamil Nadu Land Encroachment Act (Act III of 1905) in less than 5 seconds.

Key Technological Breakthrough: GEOMMAND bridges the historical "Last-Mile Enforcement Gap". While existing systems (ISRO Bhuvan, SatSure) stop at showing pixels on a screen, GEOMMAND directly arms the field officer and the Tahsildar with court-admissible statutory documents.

03. Algorithmic Formulations & Scientific Pipeline

3.1 Multi-Spectral Anomaly Differencing

The ingestion daemon computes Normalized Difference Built-up Index (NDBI) and Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) across temporal timestamps:

NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)   |   NDBI = (SWIR - NIR) / (SWIR + NIR)

A structural anomaly candidate is triggered when:

ΔNDBI = NDBI(t₁) - NDBI(t₀) > 0.22   AND   ΔNDVI = NDVI(t₁) - NDVI(t₀) < -0.18

3.2 Multi-Pass Persistence Engine (Filtering Marriage Pandals vs. Concrete)

To address temporal community events (wedding shamianas, festival tents, weekly markets) and protect vulnerable populations, GEOMMAND executes multi-temporal classification: 

def evaluate_encroachment_persistence(parcel_id, passes):
    # passes: list of satellite passes at 5-day intervals
    duration_days = len(passes) * 5
    
    # 1. Structure appears and disappears within 10 days
    if duration_days <= 10 and passes[-1].is_cleared:
        return {"status": "RESOLVED_TEMPORARY_EVENT", "action": "NO_LEGAL_NOTICE"}
        
    # 2. Structure persists >= 15 days with concrete/tin spectral signature
    if duration_days >= 15 and all(p.is_active for p in passes):
        if passes[-1].material in ["RCC_CONCRETE", "TIN_SHEET", "BRICK_MASONRY"]:
            return {"status": "PERMANENT_ENCROACHMENT", "action": "ISSUE_SECTION_7_NOTICE"}
        else:
            return {"status": "VULNERABLE_OCCUPANCY", "action": "WELFARE_REHABILITATION_ASSESSMENT"}

3.3 Mathematical Cadastral FMB Vector Reconstruction

Certified Tamil Nadu FMB sketches utilize baseline triangulation (G-lines) and perpendicular tie offsets. Given survey baseline corner stones S_A(x₁, y₁) and S_C(x₂, y₂), the baseline vector is:

G_vec = S_C - S_A   |   d_proj = ((V_i - S_A) • G_vec) / ||G_vec||
d_offset = ||(V_i - S_A) × G_vec|| / ||G_vec||

The software dynamically projects the encroached polygon vertices onto the digital FMB canvas, drawing the certified Red Hatch Encroachment Zone with offset ladder measurements.

04. Field Ground Truth & 3-Tier Anti-Bias Architecture

To guarantee legal admissibility and prevent local administrative bias, GEOMMAND integrates two hardware-enforced protocols:

  • Hardware-Enforced Haversine Geofencing: The Flutter mobile terminal calculates the real-time distance between the device's hardware GPS chip and the parcel centroid. If distance exceeds 15.0 meters, the camera shutter is locked, strictly prohibiting fraudulent desk-bound submissions.
  • 3-Tier AI Risk Triaging:
    • Tier 1 (High-Risk / Waterbody > 2,000 sq.ft): Instant auto-escalation to the District Collector and Tahsildar. Local officers cannot delete or suppress this alert.
    • Tier 2 (Medium Risk): Assigned to local VAO with a 7-day SLA timer. Negative inspection reports are subjected to random peer audits by an external Flying Squad.
